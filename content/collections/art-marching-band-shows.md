Art shows give a band a visual identity before the first note: a colour, a dot, a sketch, a stencil on a wall. The four shows here take their themes from Yayoi Kusama, Banksy, the primary colours and a painter bringing a world to life, and each one was written for a specific high school program.

Shows in this collection

Dot by Dot, written for Columbine High School in 2023, is a 6:00 intermediate show built on Kusama's infinite dots: Hindemith's Symphonic Metamorphosis, Grow Till Tall as the ballad, and Alfonso Muskedunder in the closer. Art of Rebellion, for Ola High School in 2022, is a street-art show inspired by Banksy: Bullet with Butterfly Wings, Rank & File, Everybody Wants to Rule the World and Revolution. Drawn to Life, for Travelers Rest in 2024, follows an artist sketching a world into being, with an original opener, Frank Ticheli's Blue Shades, Coldplay's Yellow and Lost It to Trying. In Living Color, for Lugoff-Elgin in 2025, is a beginner show for a small band built on True Colors, Rhapsody in Blue and Beethoven in Havana.

Why art themes work on a field

The visual program writes itself: dots, stencils, primary colours, a canvas filling in. Judges score general effect on how well music and visual agree, and an art show makes that agreement obvious.

Difficulty range

In Living Color is grade 2 to 3 for a small band. Dot by Dot and Drawn to Life are grade 3 to 4. Art of Rebellion reaches grade 5 in Rank & File and Revolution.

Buying an art show

Each show is for sale as written, as separate arrangements, or as the start of a partial custom show. The show graphics are included, which for an art show matters: the poster, the flag design direction and the colour palette come with the music. Licensing goes through Tresona and is paid by the school. Pricing is quoted per program.

Q: Do art shows need expensive props?
A: No. The original performances used flags, a floor tarp or painted boards. The music and drill do not depend on any of them.

Q: Which art show is easiest?
A: In Living Color, written at grade 2 to 3 for a small band and running 6:30.

Q: Does the show come with a visual design?
A: The show graphic and colour direction are included. Flag design, props and choreography are available on request.
