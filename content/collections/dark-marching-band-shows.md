Dark shows are the ones directors ask about in October: haunted houses, vampires, gargoyles, the deep sea. These shows carry the dark theme, from a grade 2 vampire show for a small band to an advanced show set on the ocean floor. All of them were written for real programs and performed for a full season.

Shows in this collection

The Uninvited, written for New Bern High School in 2025, folds Hush Little Baby into Bernard Herrmann's Psycho, drives through Bartók's Allegro Barbaro, turns to Alanis Morissette's Uninvited for the ballad and closes on Night on Bald Mountain. Immortal, for Rock Canyon, is a vampire show for a small band: Danse Macabre, Olivia Rodrigo's Vampire and Mozart's Dies Irae. Gargoyle, for Valley View, brings Gothic stone to life with Cape Fear, Bartók's String Quartet No. 2 and Albinoni's Adagio in G Minor. Undercurrents, for Lexington, is an advanced show about pressure and depth. Apex, Strings of Fate and Spices, Perfumes & Toxins are here too.

Why dark shows score

Contrast and pacing. A dark show earns its loud moments by being quiet first, and the ballads in this collection, Uninvited and Adagio in G Minor among them, are some of the strongest we have written. Sound design does a lot of work: a sampled heartbeat, a whispered lullaby, a low synth under the ballad.

What to watch for

Dark does not have to mean difficult. Immortal is a grade 2 show. The Uninvited and Gargoyle are grade 3 to 4. Undercurrents and Spices, Perfumes & Toxins are the advanced options. Every show here has a clear story a judge can follow in one viewing, which matters more for a dark show than for a bright one.

Buying a dark show

Each show is for sale as written, as separate arrangements, or as the start of a partial custom show. Licensing goes through Tresona and is paid by the school; some film and pop pieces take longer to clear than others, and we tell you that up front. Pricing is quoted per program.
