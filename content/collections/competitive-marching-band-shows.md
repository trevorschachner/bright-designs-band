These are the shows we wrote for bands chasing state finals and Bands of America regional placements: grade 4 to 5 winds, full percussion and sound design, and runtimes up to 8:30. These shows carry the Advanced label, including the three we wrote for Alpharetta High School in Georgia and two for Lexington High School in South Carolina.

What advanced means in our catalog

The wind books demand real technique: extended ranges, fast tonguing in the openers, exposed soloists in the ballads. Percussion writing assumes a full battery and a front ensemble of six or more. Sound design is part of the composition rather than an add-on; the synth and sample layers are written into the score.

Shows in this collection

Worlds Apart, written for Alpharetta in 2025, runs Separate Ways, Toccata Andromeda, Somewhere Only We Know and a Hyperjump closer in 8:30. No Strings Attached, also for Alpharetta, builds a marionette story out of Pinocchio and Billie Eilish's What Was I Made For?. True North, for Lexington, is the show about the aurora: Slalom, Aurora Awakes and Vuelie. Undercurrents, Spices Perfumes & Toxins, Strings of Fate, Turn It Up! and The Caterpillar round out the list.

Why buy a pre-written competitive show

A custom show at this level takes a winter of design meetings and a spring of writing. A pre-written one is finished, performed and recorded, and it can be in your folders by March. Most directors who buy one change something: a different ballad, a shorter closer, a soloist moved to the instrument they have. All of that is normal and we do it as part of the sale.

What is included

Winds, percussion, sound design files, the show graphic and the audio you hear on each page. Drill and choreography are available on request; several of these shows have drill written by our team that can be adapted to your numbers. Licensing goes through Tresona and is paid by the school. Pricing is quoted per program.

Q: Are these shows BOA-ready?
A: They were written for bands that compete at BOA regionals and state finals, with the structure and effect those judges score. How well they place depends on the band performing them.

Q: Can we buy only the winds book?
A: Yes. Every show and every arrangement can be bought on its own. Most bands at this level take winds, percussion and sound design together because they were written as one piece.

Q: Can you adapt the drill to our band size?
A: Where drill exists for a show, we can rewrite it for your numbers. Where it does not, we write new drill for the show, due by Labor Day.
