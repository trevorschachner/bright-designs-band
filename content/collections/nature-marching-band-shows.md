Nature shows run from a caterpillar becoming a butterfly to a wolf pack hunting under the moon. The shows here take their themes from seasons, animals, fire, water and the northern sky, and the range covers grade 2 to grade 5.

Shows in this collection

Feathered Flame, written for West Oak High School in 2025, is a beginner phoenix show: Mahler's Primal Light, de Falla's Ritual Fire Dance, Arcade Fire's My Body Is a Cage and Rise from the Ashes. Apex is a wolf-pack show on Hungry Like the Wolf, Bartók and Moonlight Sonata. The Caterpillar, for Green County Tech, is an advanced show from crawling to flying. 4 Seasons of Kansas, for Valley Center, follows a year on the plains. Undercurrents, for Lexington, goes under the sea. True North, also for Lexington, goes to the aurora. Head in the Clouds, for Travelers Rest, is a daydream show on Kaval Sviri, Imagine and Here Comes the Sun. Ride the Wave is a beginner surf show.

Why nature themes travel well

They do not date and they do not need explaining. A judge reading "Feathered Flame" in the program knows the arc before the first note, so the show can spend its effect on music and drill instead of exposition. Colour guard and visual design have an obvious palette: fire, water, snow, the plain.

Difficulty range

Feathered Flame and Ride the Wave are grade 2. 4 Seasons of Kansas, Apex and Head in the Clouds are grade 3 to 4. The Caterpillar, True North and Undercurrents are advanced.

Buying a nature show

Each show is for sale as written, as separate arrangements, or as the start of a partial custom show. Licensing goes through Tresona and is paid by the school. Pricing is quoted per program; tell us the show and your instrumentation and we will send a quote within a day.

Q: Can we change the ending of a show?
A: Yes. Swapping or rewriting a closer is a partial custom job and one of the most common things we do.
