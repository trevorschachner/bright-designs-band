Every show on this page was written and performed in 2025 or later, which means it is new to the circuit for the 2027 season. Nineteen shows qualify. They were custom shows for real bands first, so each one has been through a full competitive season, been recorded, and been cleaned up for resale.

Why a recent show matters

Judges and audiences remember what they saw two years ago. A show that competed in 2025 in Georgia is new to a circuit in North Carolina in 2027. The music on this page has not been performed by dozens of bands, and several shows have only been performed once.

What is new

Worlds Apart and Strings of Fate are advanced shows from 2025, one for Alpharetta and one for Indian Land. Aromatic, Gargoyle, Lunch Atop A Skyscraper, The Times, The Uninvited, Takeover and Off the Rails are the 2025 intermediate shows. Make Some Noise, The Caterpillar, True North and A Night At The Opera complete the list.

What you can check before you ask

Every show page has a recording of each part, the source pieces and composers credited movement by movement, the difficulty, the runtime and the school it was written for. Listen to the whole show on the page, then tell us which parts you would keep and which you would change.

The calendar for 2027

Directors who buy a pre-written show for next fall usually decide in December or January so the band has music before spring break. Custom shows start with concept meetings in the winter; music is due May 1 and drill by Labor Day. Either way, the earlier the conversation, the more choice you have.

Buying and changing a show

Buy the full show, mix arrangements from several shows, or use one as the start of a partial custom program. Licensing goes through Tresona and is paid by the school. Pricing is quoted per program; tell us the show and your instrumentation and we will send a quote within a day.
