## easy-marching-band-shows

Easy marching band shows are the ones we wrote for younger or smaller bands: grade 2 winds, percussion books that a first-year battery can clean, and a runtime under seven and a half minutes. Six shows in the catalog fit that description, and every one of them was performed by a real high school program before it went on sale.

Who these shows are for

Programs with a lot of freshmen, bands coming back from a rebuilding year, and directors who would rather have a clean show in October than a hard one in November. The wind parts sit in comfortable ranges, the ballads give the band room to breathe, and the closers end on something the stands can sing back.

What a grade 2 show from us sounds like

Ride the Wave, written for Greenwood High School in 2025, is a surf show built on Misirlou, the Beach Boys' Girls on the Beach, and the Chantays' Pipeline. It runs 5:48. Excalibur, for Batesburg-Leesville, pairs Mozart's Fantasia in D Minor with Purcell's Dido's Lament and John Powell's Test Drive. Pale Blue Dot opens with Hans Zimmer's Day One and Holst's Jupiter and closes on Jacob Collier's Little Blue. None of these are watered-down versions of harder shows; they were designed at this level from the first sketch.

How we keep an easy show interesting

The difficulty lives in the writing, not the parts. Short phrases get passed around the band so every section has a moment. Percussion carries the energy while winds hold sustained lines. Sound design files are included, so a synth pad or a sampled voice can fill the texture a small wind section leaves open.

Three ways to buy

- The full show as written, with winds, percussion, sound design files and the show graphic.
- Build your own: take the ballad from one show and the closer from another. Every arrangement in the catalog is available on its own.
- Partial custom: keep two parts we have already written and have us write the rest for your band.

Licensing goes through Tresona and is paid by the school; we tell you what each piece needs before you commit. Pricing is quoted per program. Tell us your instrumentation and we will send a quote within a day.

---faq---
Q: What grade level are the easy marching band shows?

## grade-3-marching-band-shows

Grade 3 is where most competitive high school bands live, and it is where most of our catalog lives: nineteen shows written at grade 3 to 4 for programs across South Carolina, Georgia, Colorado, Kansas and North Carolina. Each one was a custom show for a real band first, which means the pacing, the ballad placement and the closer have all been tested in front of judges.

What intermediate means here

The wind books use the full range of the instrument but stay inside what a strong sophomore can play with good tone. Technical passages are short and repeated rather than long and new. Percussion books assume a battery that has marched a season and a front ensemble with at least two mallet players. Runtimes sit between 6:00 and 8:45.

Shows in this collection

Aromatic, written for Gilbert High School in 2025, moves from Avner Dorman's Spices through Scheherazade to a closer that pairs Wayfaring Stranger with Saint-Saëns' Bacchanale. The Uninvited, for New Bern, is a haunted-house show that folds Hush Little Baby into Bernard Herrmann's Psycho and ends on Night on Bald Mountain. Fast Lane, for Valley Center, runs Samuel Hazo's Go!, Olivia Rodrigo's Drivers License and Danger Zone in 6:38. Gold Rush, Off the Rails, Takeover, Gargoyle, Drawn to Life and Let Them Eat Cake are all here too.

Why we write so much at this level

A grade 3 show gives a band something to grow into without a cliff in September. The groups we work with most are state finalists and bands trying to become one; those programs need a show that is clean by the first contest and still has effect left to add in October. Grade 3 writing does that.

Changing a show to fit your band

Every show on this page can be bought as written, assembled from parts of several shows, or used as the start of a partial custom program. Swapping a ballad is common; so is cutting a four-part show to three for a shorter runtime. Licensing is handled through Tresona and paid by the school. Pricing is quoted per program.

---faq---
Q: What is the difference between a grade 3 and a grade 4 show?

Q: Were these shows written for big bands?
A: No. Most were written for bands of 40 to 90 winds. A few, like Off the Rails and Gold Rush, were written for smaller groups and are tagged as small-band shows.

Q: How fast can we get the music?

## small-band-marching-band-shows

Small bands get the worst deal in show design: most catalogs are written for 80 winds and then thinned out. The shows on this page were written for bands under 40 winds from the first note. The arranging does the work a big band does by volume: short, layered phrases, percussion that carries the pulse, and sound design that fills the texture a small wind section cannot.

Shows written for small bands

Excalibur was written for Batesburg-Leesville High School in 2024, an Arthurian show in 6:27 that moves from Mozart to Purcell to John Powell. Pale Blue Dot, for the same school, is a space show built on Zimmer, Holst, Sara Bareilles and Jacob Collier. Immortal, for Rock Canyon, is a vampire show that pairs Danse Macabre with Olivia Rodrigo's Vampire and Mozart's Dies Irae. In Living Color, for Lugoff-Elgin, builds a whole show out of True Colors, Rhapsody in Blue and Beethoven in Havana.

How a small-band show is written differently

Every section gets melody. Trumpets do not carry the show alone. Low brass and saxes double in the openers so the sound stays full, and the ballads use the whole band on sustained chords instead of a thin soloist over nothing. The percussion books are written for a battery of six to ten and a front ensemble that might be two keyboards and a synth.

What sound design does for a small band

Each show ships with the sound design files we used in the original performance: bass, pads, effects and sampled voices. For a 30-piece band those files are the difference between sounding like 30 and sounding like 60. The director's guide explains which cues matter and how to balance them with the live sound.

Buying and changing a show

Buy the full show, build your own from the arrangements, or keep two parts and have us write the rest. We will rebalance any show on this page for your exact instrumentation before delivery. Licensing goes through Tresona and is paid by the school. Pricing is quoted per program.

Q: Do we need a front ensemble?
A: Each show includes a front ensemble book, but the sound design files cover the bass and pad layers, so a band with two keyboard players can still perform them fully.

## indoor-winds-shows

Indoor winds is a different show: five to six minutes, a gym instead of a stadium, a floor instead of a field, and an audience close enough to see faces. The music has to land faster and change more often. This collection holds the shows we have written for winter wind ensembles.

In The Key of Cool

Written for Travelers Rest High School's 2025 winter ensemble, In The Key of Cool is a 5:00 jazz show. Bobby Timmons' Moanin' opens it, House of the Rising Sun is the ballad, and Cory Wong's Golden closes it. The parts are written at an advanced level for a group that already had a strong fall season and wanted something with swing and space for soloists.

What we write for indoor winds

Three movements rather than four, because six minutes does not leave room for a long transition. Percussion is written for the floor: a small battery and a front ensemble that doubles as the amplified rhythm section. Sound design is heavier than in a fall show because the room is dead and the audience is close.

What the package includes

The full score and parts as PDFs, MusicXML files for edits, MP3 recordings for rehearsal, the sound design file, the show graphic and a director's guide with setup notes. Choreography for winds is available on request, which matters more indoors than on a field because the audience can see every hand.

Custom indoor shows

Most of our indoor work is custom. A winter ensemble usually starts with a specific idea, a specific soloist or a specific piece the director has wanted to use for years. We write those shows between October and January so the band has the music before winter break.

Buying a show

Every indoor show here is for sale as written or as a starting point. Licensing goes through Tresona and is paid by the school. Pricing is quoted per program.

---faq---
Q: How long is an indoor winds show?
A: Five to six and a half minutes, depending on the circuit. In The Key of Cool runs 5:00.

Q: Can you write a custom indoor winds show?
A: Yes. Most of our indoor work is custom. Contact us in September or October so the music is ready before winter break.

Q: Can a fall show be cut down for indoor?
A: Sometimes. A three-part fall show with a strong ballad can be trimmed to six minutes. We will tell you whether a specific show works before you buy it.

Why dark shows score

Dark does not have to mean difficult. Immortal is a grade 2 show. The Uninvited and Gargoyle are grade 3 to 4. Undercurrents and Spices, Perfumes & Toxins are the advanced options. How we arrange a pop song for the field

The hook has to arrive in the first eight counts and come back at the end. Between those, the arrangement goes places the recording does not: a half-time feel, a key change, a wind chorale version of the chorus. The ballads in these shows, Drivers License and Agape, are written so a band can play them softly and still fill a stadium.

Licensing pop music

Every pop and rock song in these shows needs a licence through Tresona, and some publishers take longer than others to answer. ## classical-marching-band-shows

Classical and opera shows give a band music that is out of copyright, plays well in the open air and has already survived two centuries of audiences. Shows in this collection

Why classical sources help a band

Licensing

Public-domain pieces need no licence. Arrangements of recent film and pop pieces inside these shows, like Test Drive or Love of My Life, go through Tresona and are paid by the school. We list which pieces need what before you commit.

They do not date and they do not need explaining. A judge reading "Feathered Flame" in the program knows the arc before the first note, so the show can spend its effect on music and drill instead of exposition. Colour guard and visual design have an obvious palette: fire, water, snow, the plain.

Difficulty range

Feathered Flame and Ride the Wave are grade 2. 4 Seasons of Kansas, Apex and Head in the Clouds are grade 3 to 4. The Caterpillar, True North and Undercurrents are advanced.

## story-marching-band-shows

Story shows have a plot: a worker eating lunch on a steel beam, a newsroom on deadline, a revolution, a marionette cutting its strings. Eighteen shows in the catalog carry a narrative the audience can follow from opener to closer, which is also what judges score under general effect.

Shows in this collection

Lunch Atop A Skyscraper, written for Columbine High School in 2025, follows an ironworker through a day above the city on Back to the Future, 9 to 5, Steve Reich's New York Counterpoint and New York, New York. The Times, for Houston County, is a newsroom show on the overture to Newsies, Leroy Anderson's The Typewriter and the Forrest Gump feather theme. Let Them Eat Cake, for Rock Canyon, is a revolution. No Strings Attached, for Alpharetta, is a marionette breaking free. Gold Rush, Off the Rails, Drawn to Life, Art of Rebellion, Excalibur, A Night At The Opera, That's Amore, Gargoyle, Feathered Flame, Head in the Clouds, Spices Perfumes & Toxins, Strings of Fate, Fast Lane and Aromatic are also here.

Why a story helps a band

A story gives every design decision a reason. The ballad is where the character doubts; the closer is where they win. Students learn the show faster because they know what each part means, and directors have a ready answer when a parent asks what the show is about. Judges reward coherence, and a story is the simplest way to be coherent.

Difficulty range

Excalibur and Off the Rails are grade 2. Most of the collection is grade 3 to 4. No Strings Attached, Spices Perfumes & Toxins and Strings of Fate are advanced.

Buying a story show

Each show is for sale as written, as separate arrangements, or as the start of a partial custom show. Story shows adapt well to partial custom: keep the plot and two of the movements, and have us write a new ballad for your soloist. Licensing goes through Tresona and is paid by the school. Pricing is quoted per program.
